Step 4 of 6

One decision: a discount must buy value.

I'd apply it in three places at once, so agents, people and the system all pull the same way.

Sales agents

Up to 5% on their own, and only in exchange for a longer term, prepayment, volume or a referral. Above 5%, they ask a person.

Ready in the sandbox. No extra cost.

Sellers

A new onboarding talk with their manager: lead with value, and trade any discount for something real.

Coaching time not estimated yet.

The CRM

A required “Reason for discount” field whenever a discount is set. Developer agents add it.

Ready in the demo CRM. Production cost not estimated.
