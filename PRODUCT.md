# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The hackathon team builds and demonstrates the system. The proposed product serves an owner who needs to understand and direct an organization of AI agents.

## Product Purpose

Turn raw information into a traceable model and bounded agents, while giving a human a comprehensible view of goals, rules, emergent culture, evolution and intervention.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The owner requested Meta for fact identification and structuring, a database layer, SubQ for consistency review, native agents and an option to add a Fetch.ai agent from stated intent. The exact SubQ method and application stack remain open. No runtime integration is implemented yet.

## Operating Context

Hackathon build and delivery on November 7, 2026. The reporting surface continues the previous hackathon's concept → scenarios → artifacts protocol. Its job is to show what has evidence, what remains and what blocks the demo.

## Evidence on Hand

Owner's project brief; a previous delivery protocol; reported provider readiness. No current product evaluation or end-to-end run.

## Product Principles

- Every accepted claim traces to source evidence and a review decision.
- Uncertainty and contradictions remain visible.
- Proposed structures do not silently become agent authority.
- Public progress reflects evidence, not ambition.
