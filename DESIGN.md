# Theseus design direction

## Owner-approved product identity · November 7, 2026

Use **`styles/theseus-logo-v8-spacesuit.png`**: the half-glacier, half-digital-amber Ship of Theseus, with a human in a transparent-helmet spacesuit holding the wheel. Taras accepted this version. Previous studies remain in `styles/` as process evidence; they are not the current mark.

**Landing pages and presentations inherit Firny's visual language**, as explicitly directed by the owner: snow/white surfaces, deep navy type, glacier-blue structure, generous spacing, dimensional character art and sparse meaningful connections. Typography: Geist for primary text, Instrument Serif for restrained editorial accents, Geist Mono for technical labels. Amber highlights active reconstruction. A spacesuit does not call for a dark cosmic or neon interface.

The narrative is continuity: the same evidence and organizational material can become new capabilities while human direction remains visible. Keep the human/ship relationship clear; avoid explanatory clutter around the mark.

Assets, CSS tokens, usage and exact generation records: [styles/README.md](styles/README.md). This acceptance sets direction for future landing and presentation work; it does not claim a live page, deck, public profile or upload has been changed.

## Existing reporting utility

The local delivery report remains a readable operational document: outcomes and defense levels first, then concept → scenarios → artifacts and evidence. It uses cool light `#F3F7F7`, white surfaces, dark ink `#1C1C1A`, jade `#00695E` and amber for caution. System text keeps it offline and dependency-free. Status always has a written label; color is supplementary. Tables scroll within their containers. Native details controls expose evidence by keyboard.

This reporting utility's existing palette is not the product identity. No reporting restyle or application-stack decision is implied by the branding acceptance.
