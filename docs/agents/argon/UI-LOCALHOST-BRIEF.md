## The product and six required states

The control plane commits to monitor the human's intent, goals, codex and dangerous cultural drift. It shows evidence, obtains human decisions and checks their effect.

Follow [DEMO-INTERFACES.md](../../../reports/2-scenarios/DEMO-INTERFACES.md). Four views supply six screenshots:

| Frame | View | Minimum visible interaction |
|---|---|---|
| S01 | **Commit** | Review/edit meeting-derived intent, goal and principle; approve a monitoring mandate; show version, effective time and actual coverage/state. A saved mandate is not proof of active monitoring. |
| S02 | **Observe / Culture** | Recurring behavior with count/denominator; open independent examples and counterexamples in an evidence drawer. |
| S03 | **Observe / Drift** | Applicable committed rule vs episode/pattern; evidence, scope and SubQ verdict, including insufficient evidence. |
| S04 | **Decide / Proposal** | One proposal with three diffs: agent rules, human onboarding and required discount reason in our demo CRM. |
| S05 | **Decide / Application** | Owner decision, exact candidate version, checks and application state; demo CRM form validates the required reason. Approval is separate from activation. |
| S06 | **Track** | Labeled simulation, matched before/after runs, metric denominators, intervention/version markers; actual keep/rollback state. |

Use one connected scenario and stable identifiers across all six states. Taras operates the app; the Deputy CEO explains and directs him to the relevant view when the bridge is available. Product navigation must work independently of external chat.

## Five areas: use them as a lens, not five extra screens

Source: owner-selected business 5×2 frame, version 3 (November 4), in `/Users/taraspustovoy/Projects/hackathons/2026-11-07-aie-code/FIVE-AREAS.md`.

| Area | See / preserve | Direct / change | Minimal expression in this demo |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Purpose** | Organizational picture and memory | Purpose and boundaries | Human intent, goal and rationale; source/meeting references; version history in Commit. |
| **Rules** | Observed practices, emerging culture and exceptions | Explicit principles, permissions and codex | Separate observed culture from approved rules. Show rule scope, evidence and SubQ review in Observe. |
| **Resources** | Costs, capacity and limits | Priorities and allocation | Show known time/budget/capacity constraints and the proposed change's resource implications. Unknown values stay unknown. |
| **Relationships** | Commitments and affected parties | Human voice and organizational communication | Who is affected, who is accountable, whose approval is needed; preserve the owner's ability to question or reject. |
| **Progress** | Learning and reliability | Results and delivery | Versioned interventions, check results, simulated outcomes and rollback in Track. |

Keep this compact: relevant labels and fields inside the main workflow, not a new dashboard per area. No invented probabilities of achieving goals or fabricated relationship/resource metrics.

**Cross-cutting decision states:** received → understood → applied → observed in behavior. Acknowledgment is not compliance. Use evidence for each state, with partial/unknown outcomes visible.
